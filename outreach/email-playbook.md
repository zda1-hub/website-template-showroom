# One-to-one outreach playbook

## Best lead profile

- Local, independently owned business
- Website is missing, dated, slow, confusing, or difficult on mobile
- Public business email is listed on the company website or verified profile
- The matching template would clearly improve booking, quote requests, reservations, or orders

## First email

**Subject:** Made a website direction for {{business_name}}

Hi {{owner_or_team}},

I came across {{business_name}} and noticed {{one_specific_observation}}. I build short, mobile-friendly websites for {{category}} businesses, so I wanted to show you a direction that could work well for you:

{{matching_demo_url}}

It puts {{main_customer_action}} front and center. If you like the direction, I can customize the branding, services, photos, and contact details around {{business_name}}.

Would you like me to mock up the first screen with your business name?

— {{sender_name}}

## Follow-up after 3–4 business days

**Subject:** Re: website direction for {{business_name}}

Hi {{owner_or_team}},

Just circling back in case the demo got buried. I’d be happy to put together a no-pressure first-screen preview using {{business_name}}’s branding, so you can judge the idea before deciding anything.

{{matching_demo_url}}

Worth mocking up?

— {{sender_name}}

## Category hook ideas

- Nail salon: make services and appointment booking effortless on mobile
- Barber shop: show cuts, prices, availability, and booking immediately
- Mechanic: build trust quickly and make service scheduling obvious
- HVAC: prioritize emergency calls and same-day appointment requests
- Roofing: turn local credibility into inspection and estimate requests
- Custom goods: let customers visualize a product and request a quote
- Restaurant: show atmosphere, signature dishes, hours, and reservations quickly

## Sending rules

- Personalize every first sentence.
- Link only to the matching category demo, not all seven.
- Send small reviewed batches rather than mass blasts.
- Use only public business contact details.
- Honor opt-outs and stop follow-ups after one unanswered follow-up.
